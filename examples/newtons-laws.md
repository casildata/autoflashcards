# Newton's Laws and Rockets

## Forces

- **Force**: a push or a pull on an object.
- **Mass**: the amount of matter in an object.
- **Weight** is the force of gravity on an object's mass.
- **Acceleration**: how quickly an object's speed or direction changes.
- Balanced forces cancel out, so the object's motion doesn't change.
- **Unbalanced force**: a net force that changes an object's motion.

## The Three Laws

- **First law**: an object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion
  in a straight line, unless an unbalanced force acts on it.
- **Second law**: force equals mass times acceleration (F = ma).
- **Third law**: for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.
- **Inertia** is the tendency of an object to resist a change in its motion.

## Rockets

- A rocket pushes exhaust gases out of its nozzle; the gases push the rocket forward
  (third law).
- **Thrust**: the force that moves a rocket, produced by expelling gas.
- **Propellant**: the fuel and oxidizer a rocket burns.
- A **solid propellant rocket** burns a fuel and oxidizer mixed into a solid that can't be
  turned off once lit.
- A **liquid propellant rocket** pumps fuel and oxidizer from separate tanks into a
  combustion chamber, so its thrust can be throttled.
- **Staging**: dropping empty sections of a rocket so the rest carries less mass.
